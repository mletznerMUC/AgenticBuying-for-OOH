# 10 — Sustainability

> Status: **stub** — outline only.

**Question this document answers:** can an agent take the environmental cost of an
OOH buy into account, and what data would it need?

## What this document will cover

- [ ] Emissions sources specific to OOH: screen power draw, panel illumination,
      print production, material waste, installation and maintenance logistics
- [ ] Energy per play as a function of screen size, brightness and loop length
- [ ] Grid carbon intensity by location and time of day
- [ ] Static vs digital: a very different emissions profile, not a strictly worse one
- [ ] Existing frameworks and how OOH is treated in them
- [ ] What a seller can credibly disclose, at what granularity, and with what
      verification
- [ ] Emissions as an agent optimisation objective alongside reach and cost

## Why the digital-first assumption breaks

Digital emissions frameworks model data transfer, ad serving and device power. OOH
emissions are dominated by physical screen operation and print production, which
those models do not capture. Estimating OOH with a digital model gives a wrong
answer in both directions.
