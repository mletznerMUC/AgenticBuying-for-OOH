# Glossary

> Status: **seed** — terms listed, definitions to be tightened and sourced.

OOH vocabulary with the nearest digital-advertising equivalent, where one exists.
"—" means there is no clean equivalent, which is usually where a protocol gap sits.

| Term | Meaning | Nearest digital equivalent |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Panel / frame | A single physical advertising surface | Placement |
| Screen | A single digital panel capable of playing a schedule | Placement |
| Face | One side of a structure; a structure can hold several faces | — |
| Network | A media owner's grouping of panels sold as a unit | Inventory package |
| Loop | The repeating sequence of slots on a digital screen | — |
| Loop length | Duration of one full loop (e.g. 60 s) | — |
| Slot / spot | One position in the loop (e.g. 10 s) | Ad break position |
| Play / play-out | One execution of a creative on one screen | Ad render |
| Proof of play (PoP) | Log evidencing that a play occurred on a screen at a time | Impression log |
| Share of voice (SOV) | Share of loop slots or loop time allocated to a buyer | Share of voice |
| Share of time (SOT) | SOV expressed as time rather than slot count | — |
| Impression multiplier | Modelled number of people per play | — |
| Contact / OTS | "Opportunity to see" — a modelled audience contact | Impression |
| Audience currency | Market-agreed methodology producing OOH audience figures | Measurement vendor |
| Venue type | Classified environment of a panel (roadside, transit, retail, ...) | Content category |
| Environment | Coarse grouping of venue types | — |
| Dayparting | Restricting play-out to time windows | Dayparting |
| Illumination hours | Hours a panel is lit / operating | — |
| Classic / static OOH | Printed posters and billboards | — |
| Digital OOH (DOOH) | Screen-based OOH | — |
| pDOOH | Programmatically traded DOOH | Programmatic |
| Booking period | Trading unit of time for classic OOH (e.g. calendar weeks, decades) | Flight |
| Roadblock | Owning all frames in a location or all slots in a loop | Takeover |
| Synchronised creative | One creative executed across adjacent frames in coordination | — |
| Dynamic creative / DCO | Creative varied by data trigger (weather, transit, score, countdown) | DCO |
| Copy clearance | Approval of creative by media owner, landlord or authority | Creative approval |
| Landlord | Owner of the site a panel stands on, with its own content rules | — |
| Make-good | Compensating under-delivery with additional plays or panels | Make-good |
| Footfall attribution | Measuring store visits following exposure | Conversion attribution |
| Geopath / Route / MAX | Examples of national OOH audience currencies (US / UK / DE) | — |
| OpenOOH venue taxonomy | Industry-standard venue classification, hierarchical | Content taxonomy |
