## What changed from R1.0

R1.0's additions were written against public summaries of AdCP and AAMP. R1.1 is the
same sixteen additions checked against the **actual specifications**, cloned and read.

No addition changed its requirements, so every bump is MINOR (a re-pin to a verified
upstream revision, plus added metadata — see [`../../VERSIONING.md`](../../VERSIONING.md) §3).
What changed is our understanding of how much of it already exists.

- Each addition now declares `protocol_ownership` (owner + secondary bindings) and
  `upstream_status` (`exists` / `partial` / `gap`), and carries a Verification section.
- Additions moved into directories named for their owning protocol:
  `additions/adcp/` (13) and `additions/aamp/` (3).
- Full evidence in [`../../verification/`](../../verification/).

## Headline findings

1. **OpenRTB 2.6 `Imp.Qty` already standardises the DOOH audience multiplier** — value,
   source type and measurement vendor. Ströer's `imp.ext.totalaud` is a pre-2.6
   workaround, not a standards gap.
2. **OpenRTB 2.6 has a `Dooh` object** with `venuetype`/`venuetypetax` defaulting to the
   OpenOOH taxonomy.
3. **AdCP already has `dooh` and `ooh` channels**, `property-type: dooh`,
   `dooh_metrics`, an experimental `ooh_metrics` for classic OOH, `sov_percentage` in
   DOOH pricing parameters, a creative approval state machine and a 17-value rejection
   reason-code taxonomy.
4. **OpenDirect v2.1 already has Order, Line and ChangeRequest**, with
   `preferredbillingmethod`, `leadtime` and `reservedexpirydate` (soft holds).
5. **Four confirmed gaps remain**: ADD-005, ADD-009, ADD-012, ADD-013.
6. **Two structural findings worth raising on their own**: Agentic Direct references the
   DP-AA DOOH Extension but implements none of it, and ARTF and Agentic Direct disagree
   about whether DOOH exists as an object.

## Known limitations

1. **Still `draft`.** Verified against upstream, but no addition has been reviewed by
   anyone outside this repository.
2. **AAMP only half-examined.** `buyer-agent`, `seller-agent`,
   `registry-agent-example` and `agentic-audiences` were not read. ADD-016's placement
   and the Agentic Audiences bindings of ADD-001 and ADD-015 remain unverified.
3. **The DP-AA DOOH Extension has not been read.** It is prior art for anything we take
   to Agentic Direct, and is now the highest-priority research item.
4. **Two Ströer specifications still unretrievable** — though finding 3 above largely
   dissolves the most urgent question they held.
5. **One media owner, one market.** Unchanged from R1.0.
6. **No schemas written.** The JSON in the additions remains illustrative.
